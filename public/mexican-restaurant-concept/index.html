Our story

A courtyard, a comal, and a lot of patience.

Mexican Restaurant grew up around a small kitchen herb garden — epazote, hoja santa, cilantro. Over time that garden became a restaurant, but the philosophy never changed: cook slowly, with what the season gives.

Seasonal

A menu that follows the seasons.
